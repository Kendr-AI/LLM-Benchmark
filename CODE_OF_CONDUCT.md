# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community. Benchmark disputes must focus on evidence, methods, and
claims rather than people or organizations.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and respect for different perspectives and contexts.
- Give and accept constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Disclose conflicts and distinguish measured results from advocacy.
- Respect privacy, embargoes, intellectual property, and secure disclosure.
- Correct errors publicly and preserve the historical record.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Manipulating, selectively suppressing, or misrepresenting benchmark evidence.
- Coordinating retaliation against evaluators, providers, reviewers, or users.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report community conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security
tab when confidentiality is needed. Maintainers will investigate fairly,
recuse conflicted decision-makers, document enforcement decisions, and may
remove, temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants whose conduct harms
the community. Good-faith methodological disagreement is not misconduct.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
