**Publication date:** 2026-08-08<br>

This handout publishes every result from the frozen Kendr catalog campaign. It reports 35 text-compatible endpoints and identifies two non-text catalog entries as not applicable. The results describe endpoints **as served through the Kendr API during this campaign**. They are not vendor claims, intrinsic model constants, or proof of universal superiority.

The full rank order is useful for exploration and engineering triage, but it is finer than this 15-question sample can resolve.

## How the ranking works

The primary metric is the artifact field `score_weighted_operational_goodput`. It is reported on a 0-1 scale below as a percentage. Unlike conditional quality, the operational metric conservatively retains failures and operational non-conformance in the evaluation rather than describing only successful responses.

The frozen ranking rule is:

> Conservative score-weighted operational goodput descending when available, otherwise official question-weighted objective score; objective quality, complete estimated/reported USD, then cumulative final-answer p50 latency are tie-breakers. Rank order is finer than the sample resolves. `tier` is a descriptive marginal-interval grouping only; inferential claims use paired randomization tests with Holm family-wise correction.

Column interpretation:

- **Division rank** filters the frozen overall order to one system type. It is not a separate statistical test.
- **Overall rank** is the frozen mixed-system rank from `leaderboard.json`.
- **Goodput** is the primary score-weighted operational-goodput point estimate; the parenthesized values are its artifact-reported 95% marginal interval.
- **Availability** is successful final answers divided by 15 planned questions.
- **p50 latency** is client-observed final-answer median latency under request concurrency 2. The API path was non-streaming, so time to first token is unavailable.
- **Observed cost** is the campaign total for the 15 requests, based on Kendr-reported credits converted at the configured `$0.002` per credit. `>=` means it is a lower bound because at least one failed attempt lacked complete usage or cost telemetry. `Not reported` does not mean zero cost.
- **Failures** counts unsuccessful final answers out of 15. Provider-failed `$ERROR$` observations are scored zero, while the raw call and judgment artifacts remain preserved.

## System-type divisions

Divisions prevent unlike systems from being presented as if they had identical semantics. They are assigned mechanically from the frozen catalog:

1. **Routed systems:** catalog `mode` is `intelligent`; the service may choose different underlying endpoints by request.
2. **Managed research systems:** non-routed entries whose frozen ID contains `deep-research`, or whose capability set is exactly `text` plus `web_search`.
3. **Fixed managed text endpoints:** all remaining applicable, non-routed text endpoints.

This is an operational reporting taxonomy, not a claim about a vendor's intended product category or model architecture. Cross-division overall ranks remain descriptive because routed services, research-oriented services, and fixed endpoint identifiers expose materially different behaviors.

## Statistical interpretation

The frozen leaderboard contains all `35 choose 2 = 595` pairwise comparisons. Each comparison uses a two-sided paired sign-randomization test over the shared questions. All 595 p-values are corrected as one family with Holm's procedure at family-wise alpha 0.05.

- Holm-significant comparisons: **0/595**.
- Adjacent-rank gaps separated at family-wise 5%: **0/34**.
- The declared practical-equivalence margin is +/-0.02, but failure to show a difference is not evidence of equivalence.
- Marginal 95% intervals in the tables are descriptive and are not simultaneous 95% bands over all 35 endpoints.
- A `0.0-0.0%` interval for an endpoint with 15 provider failures reflects no successful observation in this run. It does not prove that the endpoint's future or intrinsic quality is exactly zero.
- The artifact's descriptive tiers group overlapping marginal intervals. Tiers neither establish a difference nor establish equivalence.

Because all endpoints saw the same selected question IDs, paired tests are preferable to independent-sample comparisons. However, only 15 pairs are available, many observations are tied, and only one provider trial was captured per endpoint/question. These constraints produce low resolution and do not support strong ordering claims.

## Reliability, failures, and applicability

`leaderboard.json` reports no matrix-level failures: every planned endpoint produced a complete benchmark row and every row has 15 scored observations. This does **not** mean every provider call succeeded. Availability and the `Failures` column expose call-level outcomes.

Seven text endpoints returned no successful answers during the captured run: `kc-openai-o3-deep-research`, `kc-google-deep-research-preview-04-2026`, `kc-google-deep-research-max-preview-04-2026`, `kc-bedrock-anthropic-claude-fable-5`, `kc-bedrock-writer-palmyra-x4-v1-0`, `kc-bedrock-meta-llama4-maverick-17b-instruct-v1-0`, and `kc-bedrock-anthropic-claude-opus-5`. Their zero operational goodput is a real observation about the endpoint-as-served campaign, but it must not be rewritten as a claim that the underlying model cannot perform the tasks.

Other interpretation constraints:

- Provider errors count against operational performance; excluding them would answer a different, conditional-quality question.
- Failed-attempt usage telemetry was incomplete for some endpoints, so their token and cost totals are lower bounds.
- Endpoints ran in sequential blocks. Latency can include time-of-run effects and is descriptive, not a controlled load-test comparison.
- The requested output cap was the same, but failed calls with unknown usage prevent perfect cap verification for some endpoints.
- Catalog names, availability, routing policies, and provider implementations can change after the snapshot. Use the frozen IDs and hashes when citing these results.
- Provider/model licensing and publication terms must be checked before external reuse; the frozen panel records the source as `https://api.kendr.org` and does not grant new rights.

## What may and may not be claimed

Appropriate wording:

> In the frozen 2026-08-07 Kendr catalog pilot, gpt-5.5 had the highest observed score-weighted operational-goodput point estimate among fixed managed text endpoints. The 15-question experiment found no endpoint-pair difference that remained significant after Holm correction.

Unsupported wording includes "globally accepted," "certified," "universally best," or "statistically proven best." Global or standards-based acceptance would require a substantially broader pre-registered study, multilingual and jurisdictional coverage, safety and robustness tracks, repeated generations, controlled multi-region operations, independent review, and external replication.
